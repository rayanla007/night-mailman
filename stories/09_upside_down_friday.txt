In this town, every Friday, the world flips upside down.
At exactly three o'clock, gravity goes the other way.
Everyone grabs the handles on the ceiling.
Houses have doors on the roof.
Trees grow both ways.
Pizza is always eaten upside down.
For one hour, the sky is the floor.
Then at four, everything flips back.
Easy.
My little brother Milo loves Flip Friday.
He keeps his shoes on the ceiling just for fun.
But this Friday, something was wrong.
At three o'clock, the world flipped.
At four o'clock... it didn't flip back.
Five o'clock.
Six o'clock.
Midnight.
People started living on the ceiling.
Sleeping on the ceiling.
Cooking on the ceiling.
Milo was thrilled.
I was not.
Because I noticed something.
Every hour, the sky below us got a little closer.
The clouds were reaching up.
Grabbing.
Pulling.
The town was falling.
Into the sky.
I found the flip switch inside the old windmill.
It was stuck.
Someone had jammed it with a shoe.
A tiny shoe.
Milo's shoe.
"I just wanted it to last longer," he said.
I pulled the shoe out.
The world flipped back.
Everyone hit the ground.
Every Friday since, Milo keeps both shoes on.
And the sky still looks at us...
like it's hungry.
