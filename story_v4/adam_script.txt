In this town, everyone has a skip button.
It's right here, on your wrist.
Press it, and time jumps forward.
People use it to skip school.
Boring conversations.
Traffic jams.
Even heartbreak.
Your mom skips her whole drive to work.
Your teacher skips grading forty tests.
Everyone is always somewhere else.
Except you.
You're the only one whose button doesn't work.
You've pressed it a thousand times.
Nothing.
You even took it to the repair shop.
The man just laughed. "That's never happened before."
So you live through every boring minute.
Every math class.
Every long car ride.
Every awkward silence.
Kids at school call you Slow-Mo.
Your best friend Jayden thinks it's hilarious.
He skips everything.
Tests. Chores. Sundays.
He even skipped his own birthday party. Twice.
"Why would anyone live the boring parts?"
One Monday, he holds his button down.
And doesn't let go.
He skips an entire week.
Seven whole days. Gone in one blink.
His seat in class stays empty.
Monday. Tuesday. Wednesday. Thursday. Friday.
On Saturday, someone knocks on your door.
It's Jayden.
But he looks older.
His hair is longer.
His hands are shaking.
He's terrified.
He grabs your wrist.
"Don't fix your button."
"We're still awake during the parts we skip."
"Our bodies keep going. We're stuck inside, watching."
"I felt every second of that week. And I couldn't move."
"And we're not alone in there."
"Something else is driving."
Then his button blinks.
His face goes blank.
And he walks away. Smiling.
You look down the street.
Your neighbor's button is glowing.
So is the mailman's.
So is your mom's.
The whole street is skipping. All at the same time.
Their eyes are empty.
But they're all smiling.
That night, you can't sleep.
You stare at your broken button.
And for the first time ever...
it lights up.
You didn't touch it.
Your hands are under the blanket.
Someone else presses it.
