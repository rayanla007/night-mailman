# FLOAT HOUR (v3 story)

Format: ~2:15 vertical, first-person kid narrator (Leo, 12), a cut every 1–1.5 s, "This town…" hook,
escalating set pieces, comic beats, a hero solution, and a twist ending.

Cast: **Leo** (narrator, 12), **Bo** (his 6-year-old brother, never lets go of his red balloon),
**Grandpa Gus** (winds the town clock), **Mayor Grimsby** (wears a toupee), **Biscuit** (the dog).

## Script

**Act 1: the rule**
1. This town loses gravity for one minute every day.
2. At exactly twelve o'clock.
3. A siren goes off,
4. and everyone grabs the nearest railing.
5. Because if you don't... you float.
6. Cars float.
7. Dogs float.
8. Even the mayor's toupee floats.
9. Sixty seconds later, everything drops.
10. We call it Float Hour. Even though it's a minute.

**Act 2: the family**
11. I'm Leo.
12. This is my little brother, Bo.
13. Bo has never held a railing in his life.
14. He says floating is the best part of the day.
15. Our grandpa Gus winds the giant town clock every morning.
16. He told us one rule.
17. "If the clock ever stops...
18. hold on, and don't let go."
19. Bo laughed.

**Act 3: the day it broke**
20. Then came Tuesday.
21. 11:59. The siren.
22. Everyone grabbed a railing.
23. Bo grabbed his balloon.
24. Twelve o'clock. We floated.
25. Ten seconds. Thirty. Sixty.
26. Nothing came down.
27. The clock had stopped.
28. And everything kept going up.
29. Cars. Mailboxes. The school bus.
30. Bo waved at me... and kept rising.
31. I screamed his name.
32. Grandpa yelled, "The key! Someone stole the key!"
33. Without the key, the clock can't be wound.
34. And without the clock, there's no gravity at all.

**Act 4: the mayor**
35. That's when I saw him.
36. Mayor Grimsby. Holding the key.
37. "No more Float Hour," he said.
38. "From now on... it's Float Forever!"
39. His new sky hotel opens tomorrow.

**Act 5: the rescue**
40. So I let go.
41. I pushed off a lamppost and flew straight at him.
42. Biscuit flew with me.
43. Biscuit bit the toupee.
44. The mayor dropped the key.
45. I caught it.
46. Gravity: three percent.
47. I grabbed Bo's shoelace on the way up.
48. Grandpa held the clock door open.
49. The keyhole was taller than me.
50. We all pushed. Once. Twice. Three times.
51. Tick.
52. Everything came down. Really fast.
53. The mayor landed in the fountain.
54. His toupee landed on Biscuit.

**Act 6: the twist**
55. Now the key hangs around Grandpa's neck.
56. And Bo holds a railing. Mostly.
57. But last night, I woke up at 3 a.m.
58. My bed was touching the ceiling.
59. And the clock tower said twelve o'clock.
60. Someone else has a key.
61. Hold on.
