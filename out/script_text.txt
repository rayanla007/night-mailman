My town has 412 houses. And at midnight, every single one has a light on. Not because we're scared of the dark. Because of what delivers the mail. We moved in on a Tuesday. Me, my little brother Theo... ...and Biscuit, the world's worst guard dog. Our neighbor, Mr. Pell, didn't say hello. He just pointed at our windows... ...and said, 'Keep one on. Always.' Theo laughed. Theo laughs at everything. That night, I found the town rules taped inside our mailbox. Rule one: keep a light on at midnight. Rule two: never open a letter with no stamp. Rule three... was torn off. 11:58 PM. Theo hates sleeping with the light on. So he did the dumbest thing ever. Click. Biscuit started growling. Biscuit never growls. Twelve o'clock. Something slid under the door. A letter. No stamp. Theo opened it anyway. It said: Your light is off. Come out and see ours. Then I heard the window creak. I ran to his room. His bed was empty. And outside, a tall man with a mail sack was walking away... ...holding Theo's hand. I screamed. Mr. Pell was already at our door. Don't chase him. Turn your light back on. I slammed the switch. Every light in Hollow Creek flickered. And the footsteps... stopped. In the morning, Theo was back in bed. He doesn't remember anything. But Biscuit won't go near him anymore. And every night, at 11:59... ...he asks me to turn off the lamp. Last night, I found the rest of rule three. If they come back... they're not yours. Keep your light on.
